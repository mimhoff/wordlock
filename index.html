How to Play

Yes, it's another Wordle! I hope you like it.

Guess the 5-letter word in 8 tries.

Color Feedback

After each guess, the tiles change color:

  • Green: Letter is correct and in the right position
  • Yellow: Letter is in the word but wrong position
  • Gray: Letter is not in the word
  • Red: Locked letter - must be the same as the letter directly above it

Each row (starting from row 2) has one position that locks to your previous guess. A lock icon shows which position will be locked.

Tips

  • Use common vowels and consonants in your first guess
  • Pay attention to lock icons - they show where letters will be locked on the next row
  • Plan ahead! Your early guesses constrain your later options
  • Switch to Practice mode to play unlimited games
