How to Play

Guess the 5-letter word in 8 tries.

Color Feedback

After each guess, the tiles change color:

  • Green: Letter is correct and in the right position
  • Yellow: Letter is in the word but wrong position
  • Gray: Letter is not in the word

The WordLock Twist

Red tiles are locked letters.

In rows 2-7, one letter position is randomly locked. A locked letter must be the same as the letter directly above it from your previous guess.

Plan your guesses carefully - your early word choices will constrain later guesses!

A lock icon shows which position will be locked before you start typing each row.
