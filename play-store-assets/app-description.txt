🔒 WordLock - The Strategic Word Puzzle Game

Think you've mastered daily word puzzles? WordLock adds a strategic twist that will test your word-guessing skills in a whole new way!

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Guess the 5-letter word in 8 tries, just like your favorite word game. But here's the catch: starting from row 2, one position in each row "locks" - forcing you to use the same letter from your previous guess in that spot!

• Green tiles: Correct letter in the right position
• Yellow tiles: Letter is in the word but wrong position
• Gray tiles: Letter is not in the word
• Red tiles with a padlock: Must match the letter directly above it

🎯 THE STRATEGY

The lock mechanic transforms the game from pure deduction into strategic planning:
• Your early guesses constrain your later options
• Locking in a correct (green) letter is powerful
• Locking in a wrong letter makes things challenging
• Every guess matters more than ever!

💡 TIPS

• Lock icons show which position will be locked on the next row
• Plan ahead - your first guesses set up your later ones
• Try to lock in correct (green) letters for an advantage
• The best players think 2-3 moves ahead!

🎲 TWO GAME MODES

DAILY MODE: One puzzle per day, just like the classic. Compare scores with the community and track your streak!

PRACTICE MODE: Unlimited games whenever you want. Perfect for learning strategies or just having fun. Share a puzzle's link and your friends can play the exact same one.

🧠 TWO DIFFICULTIES

STANDARD: See every lock from the start and plan your route.
HIDDEN LOCKS: A lock only appears when you reach its row. No planning ahead!
